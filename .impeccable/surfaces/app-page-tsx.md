# Surface: home (one-page site)

Mode: Persuade. Visitor: Northeast Ohio homeowner, office manager, or builder arriving from Google Maps, mostly on a phone. Action: call Shellie or send a quote request (SMS/email handoff). Proof: 5.0 Google (12), BBB A+ (from her card), insured, workers' comp. Constraints: no prices, no hours schedule, no invented reviews; stock photos labeled and listed for replacement.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The site is SS Cleaning's carbon-copy work-order book; the quote request is the numbered work order itself, sitting in the first viewport. Refuses the photo hero + three icon cards + testimonial carousel every cleaner ships.

OWN-WORLD: Cool bond-white sheet on a navy desk; canary and pink carbon copies offset beneath; navy pre-printed ink in condensed caps; blue ballpoint handwriting for entries; one red numbering-machine serial; perforation rules with punch holes; ruled boxes and checkboxes. Nothing rounded or glossy.

STORY: Visitor understands who/what/where in one line, believes it through four plain facts, and either calls or fills the order and sends it by text or email.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Desktop: left 5/12 headline (Clean homes, offices & job sites across Northeast Ohio), call button + quote link, four proof facts; right 7/12 the work-order sheet (No. 0504) with live fields and send actions, copies peeking under it. Mobile: headline, call button, then the sheet full-width; sticky bottom Call / Quote bar.

FORM: Contractor work order (my list position 1, IMPECCABLE'S PICK); seed 803699cd. Signature interaction: typed entries render in ballpoint, checks draw in; on send the top sheet tears along the perforation revealing the canary "your copy".

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
