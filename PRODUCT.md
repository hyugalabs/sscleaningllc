# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Next.js (App Router) + TypeScript + Tailwind CSS + lucide-react. Pinned by the user. The user deploys to Vercel themselves.

## Users

Homeowners, property managers, office/business owners, and builders/contractors in Northeast Ohio (Portage, Summit, and Stark County; business based in Atwater, OH) who need a cleaner and are comparing options, often on a phone, often straight from a Google Maps search. Secondary audience: Shellie Kiser, the owner, who will see this page as a spec pitch before she has agreed to a website.

## Product Purpose

A one-page site for SS Cleaning LLC that turns a Google Maps click into a quote request or a phone call. Today the listing's website link loops back to Facebook; this page replaces that dead end. Success: a visitor knows within seconds what SS Cleaning does, where, and that it is trustworthy, then calls or sends a quote request.

## Positioning

Owner-operated local cleaner that covers three distinct jobs (residential, commercial/office, post-construction) and is insured with workers' comp, which matters to builders and businesses hiring for job sites.

## Operating Context

Visitors arrive from Google Maps / local search, mostly on mobile. The owner handles leads personally by phone, text, and email. There is no booking software.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Quote form composes the request and hands off to SMS (234-233-0504) or email (seamanshellie@gmail.com). No backend.
- Click-to-call everywhere.
- Hours: Google shows "Closes 5 PM"; full weekly hours unknown — do not publish a schedule.
- Pricing unknown — do not publish prices.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: SS Cleaning LLC. Owner: Shellie Kiser.
- Existing business card: navy/blue palette, "SS" monogram with house roofline and broom, sparkle motifs, tagline "Professional Cleaning Services You Can Trust", card line "From homes to businesses and new construction, we're here to help keep your space clean, fresh, and ready to enjoy."
- Voice: plain, friendly, local, owner-direct.

## Evidence on Hand

- Google: 5.0 stars, 12 reviews (review text not available — do not invent quotes).
- Business card displays BBB A+ Accredited badge (claimed by the owner's own card).
- Facebook intro: Insured, Workers comp, Residential, Post Construction, Commercial/Office.
- Facebook: ~150 followers; LGBTQ+ friendly per Google listing.
- No real job photos available to us; stock photos are used and must be listed for replacement.
- No testimonials, client names, years in business, or counts beyond the above. Do not fabricate.

## Product Principles

1. Call or quote is never more than one thumb-reach away.
2. Only true claims; proof comes from the real rating, insurance, and BBB.
3. Speak like a local owner, not a franchise.
4. Mobile-first: the Google Maps visitor is on a phone.
